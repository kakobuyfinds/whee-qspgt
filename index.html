04 / Wheebuy spreadsheet product links

What a useful entry includes.

Use consistent fields for every product. A link and an attractive photo leave too much unanswered.

Product-link review checklist
Field What to record Why it matters
Source & variant Original URL, seller, exact color and selected size Different variants can have different prices, materials or availability.
Size information Garment measurements or the model-specific footwear size chart Letter sizes and regional shoe-size conversions are not enough by themselves.
Price reference Currency, item amount, date checked and excluded fees Spreadsheet values may be outdated or exclude delivery.
QC evidence Clear photos of the actual item, labels, seams and relevant measurements QC photos help inspect visible condition but do not prove authenticity.
Seller terms Returns, exchanges, dispatch conditions and support details Terms affect your options if the item is unsuitable.
Service support Whether the chosen shopping service accepts the exact source link and item Compatibility should be confirmed directly before payment.

05 / Wheebuy shopping guide

The item price is only the beginning.

A useful shopping budget accounts for the whole journey. For international purchases, the payable total depends on the seller, shopping service, parcel characteristics, destination and applicable charges.

Compare quotes using the same destination and shipping assumptions. If parcel weight or dimensions are unknown, treat the shipping figure as an estimate.

  • Item + domestic deliveryCheck whether the seller charges separately to send the item to a warehouse.
  • Service + payment costsReview handling fees, optional services and currency conversion.
  • International shippingConfirm the quoted route, parcel restrictions and chargeable-weight rules.
  • Destination chargesCheck applicable duties, taxes and carrier handling fees for your destination.

07 / Clear answers

Before you browse.

Practical answers for using product lists and checking shopping details.

What is a Wheebuy spreadsheet?

A Wheebuy spreadsheet is a product-link list used to organize shopping finds. Useful entries include a source link, product description, price reference, sizing notes and quality-control information. This page is an independent guide and category directory, not a live spreadsheet.

How do I use Wheebuy spreadsheet product links?

Choose a category, inspect the source listing and verify the exact style, size, color, seller terms and current price. Confirm whether the chosen shopping service supports the source link before paying.

Does a spreadsheet price include shipping?

Do not assume it does. A listed amount may cover only the item. Check domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping, currency conversion and applicable import charges separately.

Can QC photos prove that a branded item is authentic?

No. Quality-control photos can help inspect visible details, sizing and condition, but they do not establish authenticity. For Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès products, verify provenance and use authorized retailers or a reputable authentication process.

Is this an official Wheebuy website?

No. This is an independent shopping guide. Category links lead to CAFANS. No affiliation with Wheebuy or the mentioned fashion brands is claimed.
